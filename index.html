What we do

LLMs inside real workflows

We build the model into a task people already have to do, with the right data, rules and checks around it, so it produces something usable and not a generic answer.

AI agents that run a process

An agent does more than reply. It plans the next step, asks a follow-up, keeps track of what was said and produces a result at the end.

Interviews in narrow sectors

We prepare candidates for interviews and run them, in fields with their own vocabulary, regulations and unwritten expectations. The questions come from practitioners, and answers are judged against what experts in that field say.
